Valokuvaskanneri – Privacy Policy

Updated: 2 October 2026

Overview

Valokuvaskanneri is an Android app for scanning printed photos with the phone camera. The developer is Jari-Matti Honkanen. This policy explains how the app handles data.

In short

The app does not collect, transmit or share any data. All photos and settings stay on your device.

What the app handles

  • Camera: the app asks for camera permission so you can photograph printed photos. Images are not sent anywhere.
  • Scanned photos and albums: photos, their thumbnails and album information are stored in the app's private storage on your device. You can delete them in the app or by uninstalling it.
  • Settings: theme, accent colour and other preferences are stored on your device.
  • Export: when you export or share photos, for example to your phone's gallery, they go where you choose. The app itself does not upload them.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect personal data or usage analytics.
  • It does not show advertising.
  • It does not use user accounts.
  • It does not send data to servers or to third parties.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page and the update date will be changed.
